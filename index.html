Safety network concept

Your friends feel it when you don't feel safe.

A cute, customizable band that learns your normal heartbeat and sweat, notices when your body says something is wrong, and quietly buzzes the friends you chose. Every band in your circle is part of one private safety network: they find each other, compare notes, and pass your alert along even when your phone has no signal.

The network

One band protects you. A circle of bands protects each other.

Each band learns its own wearer. Together they form a small private network that finds its members, shares only simple signals, and relays an alert when one phone is out of reach.

Beacon Band is a placeholder name for a concept demo. People, readings, dates and recordings shown are sample data, not real. A real product would need clinical testing, consent from everyone in a circle, and compliance with local recording and emergency-services rules. The network features (private circle links, anonymous hellos, relaying alerts without signal) are an idea being explored and have not been built or tested. The optional spray layer would only be offered where pepper spray is legal, and it never releases automatically.
